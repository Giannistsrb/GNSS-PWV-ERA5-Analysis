# GNSS Meteorology Theory


## Zenith Total Delay (ZTD)

The GNSS signal propagating through the atmosphere experiences a delay due to atmospheric refractivity.

The total delay is expressed as:

ZTD = ZHD + ZWD


where:

ZHD:
Zenith Hydrostatic Delay

ZWD:
Zenith Wet Delay


The hydrostatic component is mainly controlled by atmospheric pressure, while the wet component is related to atmospheric water vapour.
